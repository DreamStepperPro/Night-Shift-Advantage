# Privacy Policy for Night Shift Advantage

**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Developer / Entity:** Cliftworks  
**App Name:** Night Shift Advantage  

Cliftworks ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Night Shift Advantage mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

## 1. Core Philosophy: Local-First Data
Night Shift Advantage is designed as a **local-first application**. By default, all operational data—including machine check records, shift logs, density values, and target weights—is stored securely and entirely on your local device using an embedded database (SQLite/Drift). Your day-to-day production data remains on your hardware and is not transmitted to us.

## 2. Information We Collect
We do not collect, harvest, or track personal data. We only process the minimal operational data required to make the services work:
* **Operational Input:** Scale weights, machine asset numbers (e.g., 4635, 4636), active lanes, and product mode configurations entered during your shift.
* **Authentication / Sync Metadata:** If you utilize optional cloud synchronization features, technical identifiers (such as anonymous authentication tokens) may be used strictly to link your session.

## 3. Data Transfers and Google Firebase
* **No Selling of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties under any circumstances.
* **Firebase Infrastructure:** Whenever cloud synchronization or data transfer is triggered, data is routed through **Google Firebase**. 
* **User Control:** You retain the power to grant or revoke connection to the Firebase cloud at any time directly within the application settings. When disconnected, the app operates 100% locally.

## 4. Limitation of Liability (Third-Party Services)
Night Shift Advantage relies on Google Firebase infrastructure for cloud-based data storage and synchronization features when enabled. **Cliftworks is not responsible or liable for any issues, data loss, security vulnerabilities, outages, or service disruptions on Google's or Firebase's end.** Use of cloud features implies acknowledgement of Google's respective terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our app is an industrial food production tool designed for plant-floor operators and is not directed at children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date.
